Consensus Auditory-Perceptual Evaluation of Voice (CAPE-V)

Digital rating practice · Single voice sample

Listen to the recording and move each slider to mark your rating. MI = mildly deviant, MO = moderately deviant, SE = severely deviant (qualitative anchors, not fixed cutoffs). Select C = consistent or I = intermittent. When finished, select Print / Save as PDF and upload the saved PDF to Canvas.
